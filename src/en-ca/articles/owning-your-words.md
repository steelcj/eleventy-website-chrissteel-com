Sovereign publishing means that the files, the address and the decisions stay with the person who wrote the words. A platform can change its rules overnight; a folder of files on your own machine cannot.

None of this needs to be complicated. A static site can be copied to any host, moved in an afternoon and rebuilt years later from the same source.
