A page that starts as well-structured text is most of the way to being accessible. Headings say what comes next, links say where they lead, and the reader's own settings decide the size and colour of the words.

Accessibility added at the end costs more and works less well. Accessibility that comes with the format costs almost nothing, and it keeps working as the site grows.
